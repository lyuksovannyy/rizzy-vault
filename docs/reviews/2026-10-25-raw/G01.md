### G01-10 [major] Owner decision 2 drops 2FA from recovery and names email notification as the safeguard, but no Accepted ADR defines outbound mail
- Type: E / A
- Where: docs/adr/0008-account-recovery.md owner decision 2 (line 70), point 5 (line 41), point 6 step 7 (line 52); docs/adr/0045-recovery-pending-notification.md l.50; crates/rizzy-domain-auth/src/recovery.rs:24

Top 5 by importance:
1. **G01-9** (0008): a 0-hour recovery wait is accepted on any instance. The THREAT_MODEL mitigation ("only on single-account instances") is an open owner question (Q-15), and the code says it does not enforce it, so whoever holds a printed kit can take over at once.
2. **G01-10** (0008): owner decision 2 removed 2FA from recovery and named email notification as the safeguard, but no Accepted ADR defines outbound mail or an account email. Web-only accounts get no warning during the 72 h wait.
3. **G01-6** (0006/0022): the revoked-device rotation race (AR-18) is assigned to an "M3 device-management ADR" that does not exist. ADR 0075, the M3 device ADR, declines it, while revocation is scheduled for M3.
4. **G01-20** (0013): the "only these" list of secrets that may cross the binding omits bearer tokens, request signatures, the 2FA enrolment secret and import passwords. The sans-I/O design requires them to cross, and the code already passes the bearer token to JS with no stated rule.
5. **G01-4** (0003): "Signup is invite-only by default" (M1) depends on Proposed ADR 0087 (M3). The server ships a third, unspecified default (`Closed`), and CRYPTO §5.9 and ROADMAP disagree on the milestone.

Next in importance: G01-8 (0007 point 6 "backup files are JSON" contradicts the binary containers of Accepted ADRs 0023/0071, and the CRYPTO.md:809 edit 0071 promised was not made) and G01-11 (cargo-vet, decided for M1, absent and untracked).

## Unverified suspicions

- **Web-vault-only revocation.** CRYPTO.md §11.8 step 0 lets only "a remaining device (kind 1–3, unlocked, fresh re-auth)" suspend a device, and ADR 0075 l.15 needs "another durable device". A user whose only native device was stolen may then have no way to revoke it short of recovery. I did not check whether some other path exists.
- **Reconciliation epoch (0012 §7, up to 30 days).** The server accepts any `account-state` that verifies against the identity key with a higher `state_seq`. A device revoked after the backup under a *standard* rotation (identity key not rotated, 0006 owner decision 1) might publish a state that re-lists itself. Not analysed against ADR 0032.
- **Version-vector growth from ephemeral web-vault devices** (0012 Negative, "measure it in M3"). It is bounded only by the u16 wire maximum (`MAX_ENTRIES = 65_535`, crates/rizzy-sync/src/vv.rs:95). No measurement or policy was found.
- **Out of scope (for other reviewers):** ADR 0048 l.46 names `0007_storage_account_usage.sql`, but the tree has `0006_storage_account_usage.sql`. ADR 0018 l.317 names replaced parts of 0012 by pointing at its own sections rather than writing "full text that replaces the part" (0020 point 9). The deny.toml:70 comment "no system OpenSSL in the container image" looks stale against ADR 0077 §8. 0009 owner decision 1's OpenSSL image-size cost, due "in M3", is recorded as "not measured yet" (0009 l.310).
